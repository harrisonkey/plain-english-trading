The 5 Hard Gates — every trade must pass all of these
✓
SPY trend gate — Only calls when market is going up. Only puts when market is going down. Never fight the direction of the overall market.
✓
30–45 day expiry only — No weekly options (they decay too fast). No 90+ day options (too much capital tied up). Only the sweet spot.
✓
1.5x volume spike minimum — The stock must be trading 50% more than usual. Unusual activity means someone is making a real bet.
✓
Price $5–$80 only — Under $5 stocks have poor or no options. Over $80 ties up too much premium per contract. Sweet spot only.
✓
Small cap focus — Companies under $2B market cap where a 20-40% move is realistic and options are priced cheaply.
The 2 Rules You Must Follow — no exceptions, ever
Take profit at +100%
When your option doubles in value — sell. Don't wait for more. A 100% winner and a 50% loser together is still profitable. Getting greedy and holding turns winners into losers.
Stop loss at -50%
When your option loses half its value — sell immediately. No exceptions. No "let me wait and see." The number one mistake beginners make is holding losers too long hoping they come back.
Position sizing — the 5% rule
Never put more than 5% of your account into a single options trade. On a $5,000 account that's $250 maximum per position. This is the rule that makes sure one bad trade never wrecks you. The app calculates this automatically for your account size.
